Why we exist

Knowledge that changes outcomes.

Too many young people and first-generation entrepreneurs in South Florida reach adulthood, or open a business, without ever being taught how money, credit, taxes and business planning really work. Max Family Foundation teaches it directly — practically, in two languages, and with fees kept modest and waived for those who cannot pay.

01

Practical, not theoretical

Every course ends with something participants can use: a personal budget, a credit plan, a business plan, a community project.

02

Taught by practitioners

Instructors and mentors are professionals who have built businesses and advised owners, serving as volunteers alongside screened guest speakers.

03

Open to everyone

Programs are open to the public without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age or disability, with confidential fee waivers.

Our approach

We measure what participants can do, not just who attended.

  • Assess. A short knowledge assessment before each course.
  • Teach and practice. Hands-on sessions built around real decisions.
  • Apply. Participants complete a budget, plan or project.
  • Measure. A post-course assessment and satisfaction survey.
  • Follow up. A six-month check-in on budgets kept, credit improved and businesses formed.

Transparency

Policies and public information

Nondiscrimination

Max Family Foundation, Inc. admits participants of any race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age or disability to all the rights, privileges, programs and activities generally made available to participants, and does not discriminate on those bases in its educational programs, scholarships, fee waivers or other programs.

Privacy

We collect only the information needed to run our programs and meet legal obligations. We do not sell, rent or exchange personal information and do not share it with any business for marketing. Information about participants under 18 is collected only with a parent's or guardian's consent.

Youth protection

Everyone who works with participants under 18 completes a fingerprint-based background check and youth-protection training, and no adult is ever alone with a minor participant. Suspected child abuse, abandonment or neglect must be reported to the Florida Abuse Hotline at 1-800-96-ABUSE.

Public disclosure

Our application for recognition of exemption and our three most recent annual returns (Form 990-PF) are available for public inspection at our office and on request, as required by Section 6104(d) of the Internal Revenue Code.
